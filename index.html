ABOUT AECC

Built around technical capability, project delivery and field experience.

Afghan Engineers Group Engineering Services & Construction Company (AECC) is an Afghanistan-based engineering and construction company. The supplied company profile describes capabilities in construction, design, survey, logistics, project management, water engineering and civil engineering structures.

AECC’s documented portfolio includes roads, canals and irrigation works, wells, schools, government facilities, border-police facilities, custom-house facilities and building projects.
